Isra Abbasi is a Chartered Engineer (CEng), Project Management Professional (PMP®), and TÜV Rheinland Certified Functional Safety Engineer with over 11 years of experience in the oil and gas industry, specializing in Instrumentation & Control (I&C), Project Engineering, Industrial Automation, and Remote & Autonomous Operations.

Currently serving as Senior Executive, Remote & Autonomous Operations at PETRONAS, she leads engineering and digital transformation initiatives that enhance production efficiency, operational reliability, and asset performance. Her expertise encompasses the complete engineering project lifecycle, from conceptual development and Front-End Loading (FEL) to detailed engineering, commissioning, and operational handover.

Her professional contributions include delivering automation and remote operations solutions that have achieved up to 10% production uplift and approximately 30% operational expenditure reduction. She was recognized with the **2026 Offshore Technology Conference (OTC) Asia Emerging Leader Award** and two **2026 Institution of Engineers Malaysia (IEM) Pinnacle Awards** for engineering excellence and innovation.

Beyond her industry responsibilities, Isra actively contributes to professional development, engineering leadership, and academia through her involvement with the International Society of Automation (ISA), university teaching and examination, industry mentoring, technical publications, and international conference presentations.

Her professional interests include **Remote & Autonomous Operations, Industrial Automation, Functional Safety, Predictive Maintenance, Process Control, and Digital Transformation in the Energy Industry**.
